# Copy rules — majedtuah.com

Drawn from the first screens of five well-known AI engineer sites (karpathy.ai, eugeneyan.com,
hamel.dev, jxnl.co, huyenchip.com) compared with a template-style portfolio. These are the things the
strong ones all do and the weaker one doesn't. Every word on the site follows them.

## Headlines
1. **Lead with a person, not a slogan.** The first line is the name or a first-person sentence
   about the work. ✅ "I like to train deep neural nets on large datasets." ❌ "Building AI-Powered Systems That Scale."
2. **Name the employer, the product or the thing.** Concrete nouns beat categories.
   ✅ "I lead AI engineering at AventraGroup." ❌ "Enterprise-grade AI solutions."
3. **One idea per screen.** Each section makes one point, in one sentence under its heading.
   If a paragraph needs "and also", it is two screens.
4. **First person, everywhere.** "I built", "I teach". Never third person, never "we" for solo work.

## Proof
5. **Exact facts in plain sentences.** A real number with its unit, an employer, a course, a repo.
   ✅ "5,000+ students from 500+ companies." ❌ "0+ Projects" counters or rounded brag stats.
6. **No number we can't stand behind.** Impact figures only appear once they're real (see `IMPACT` in `src/data.ts`).

## Buttons and links
7. **One filled button per screen.** Everything else is a quiet secondary (glass) button or a text link.
8. **Labels say what happens, in 1–3 words:** "See my work", "Email me", "Download CV", "GitHub ↗".
   ❌ "Let's Talk", "Hire Me", "Let's Build Something Amazing".
9. **No availability badges, chat widgets or urgency.**

## Cards and lists
10. **Title + one plain line saying what it is or why it exists.** Write it the way you'd say it out loud.
    ✅ "lazynlp (building massive text datasets)." ❌ "Leveraging cutting-edge NLP to unlock insights."
11. **A metric only if it is real and measured.** Otherwise describe the purpose, not the impact.
12. **Tags are tools people search for** (Azure AI Search, PyTorch), not adjectives.

## Words we don't use
leverage · seamless · cutting-edge · robust · empower · harness · unlock · innovative · passionate ·
results-driven · scalable solutions · enterprise-grade · actionable insights / intelligence · delve ·
transformative · synergy · game-changer · next-generation · state-of-the-art · world-class · journey ·
elevate · streamline · revolutionise · AI-powered · intelligent tomorrow · something amazing ·
proven track record · drive / driving value

**Test before shipping a line:** would you say it to an engineer across a table? If not, rewrite it.
